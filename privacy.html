Freedial — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Freedial has no servers and no accounts. It does not collect, sell or transfer any personal data.

What is stored, and where

  • Your speed-dial bookmarks and groups are ordinary Chrome bookmarks in Other bookmarks › Freedial. If you turn on Chrome sync, Google syncs them like any other bookmark.
  • Preferences are stored in chrome.storage.sync (synced by Chrome when sync is on).
  • Images you upload, page screenshots, visit counts, tasks, notes, calendar links, daily backups and cached weather/calendar data are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local).

Network requests Freedial makes

  • Site logos: https://www.google.com/s2/favicons with the address of the site whose logo is shown.
  • Fonts: https://fonts.googleapis.com, only if you pick a Google Font.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com, geocoding-api.open-meteo.com), only if you turn the weather widget on — the city you chose is sent.
  • Calendar: the iCal address you paste, fetched directly from your calendar provider.
  • Page titles and page screenshots: the bookmarked page itself, only when you add a bookmark or ask for a page capture.

Permissions

  • Bookmarks — to store your dials. Storage, unlimited storage — preferences and images. Favicon — show site icons.
  • Search — send what you type to your default search engine when you choose "Search the web".
  • Context menus, active tab, scripting — "Save to Freedial" and the Jump command bar on the page you are viewing, only when you invoke them.
  • Alarms — daily backup and Focus Mode schedule. Declarative Net Request — redirect sites you chose to block in Focus Mode.
  • Optional, asked only when you use the feature: tabs, sessions (Jump and the sidebar show open and recently closed tabs), and access to sites (page capture, page titles, Focus Mode).

What Freedial reads with these permissions never leaves your browser.

Group sharing puts the group's titles and addresses inside the share link itself, after #; browsers do not send that part to any server.
